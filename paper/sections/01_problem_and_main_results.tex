\section{Problem and main results}
\label{sec:introduction}
Minimum decycling sets (MDS) remove all directed cycles from a de Bruijn graph at minimum cardinality. They support sequence sketching; the ordinary minimum follows from Mykkeltveit's construction and the unavoidable-set formulation of Champarnaud, Hansel and Perrin \cite{Mykkeltveit1972,ChamparnaudHanselPerrin2004}. Recent work studies residual paths, move components and reverse-complement-aware sketches \cite{ZhengKingsfordMarcais2021,PellowEtAl2023,MarcaisDeBlasioKingsford2024,MarcaisElderKingsford2024}.

The imported parity construction \cite{Babanskyy2026Parity} admits arbitrary injective, nonzero, antisymmetric real weights. Our complete DNA atlas and its 141 bound concern precisely the fixed labelled family
\[
 \mathcal W=\{(s(A),s(C),s(G),s(T))=(-\rho,-1,1,\rho):\rho>1\},
\]
with the fixed lexicographic tie order $A<C<G<T$. The general labelled weight domain is larger and is not classified here. Length $L$ counts residual arcs in the lower graph $B(q,k-1)$; \Cref{sec:base} specifies the equivalent upper-graph and avoiding-word conventions.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-family separation at DNA order eight]
\label{thm:headline-separation}
With these labels and ties, the terminals of $\mathcal W$ meet exactly 21 ordinary complete-move components. The minimum of $L$ over the union of these entire components is 141, including non-RC endpoints. A reverse-complement-invariant MDS with 8230 words instead has $L=107$, lies in a different component, and attains that component's optimum. There are also RC feedback sets with 8232 words and $L=105$. If $\tau_{\RC}(106)$ is the minimum cardinality of an RC feedback set at residual path bound 106, then
\[
 8230\le\tau_{\RC}(106)\le8232.
\]
Neither the global minimum length of an RC MDS nor the exact value of $\tau_{\RC}(106)$ is determined.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The atlas and component coverage are \Cref{prop:family-components}; the complete-component optima are \Cref{prop:component-optima}. The 107 witness and its component certificate are \Cref{thm:construction-107}. The cardinality statements and their evidence are developed in \Cref{prop:8232}. Since 107 is below 141, its component is outside the union.
\end{proof}

Thus varying $\rho$ in $\mathcal W$ and then applying complete moves and inverses cannot reach the 107 witness. This does not exclude all other antisymmetric weight assignments.

\paragraph{Why the labels matter.}
At $k=4$, keep the same ties and compare $s_0=(-2,-1,1,2)$ with $s_1=(-2,1,-1,2)$. On the simple directed cycle
\[
\texttt{AAAC}\to\texttt{AACA}\to\texttt{ACAG}\to\texttt{CAGA}\to\texttt{AGAA}\to\texttt{GAAA}\to\texttt{AAAC},
\]
the first terminal selects only \texttt{AGAA}; the second selects \texttt{ACAG} and \texttt{AGAA}. Cycle sums of a gradient telescope, so these terminals belong to different ordinary complete-move components. A weight relabelling cannot silently be absorbed into $\mathcal W$ while the ties stay fixed.

\paragraph{Contribution and mathematical dependencies.}
The chain is weights, selector, chambers, components, escape from the fixed-family union, and limits of exterior relaxations. The two-family selector includes equality strata. Cycle-signature component invariance is due to Mar\c cais, DeBlasio and Kingsford \cite{MarcaisDeBlasioKingsford2024}; our component optimization specializes classical retiming \cite{LeisersonSaxe1991}. The two RC sectors use the tight-edge SCC criterion for UTVPI systems \cite{LahiriMusuvathi2005}, with Boolean endpoint reconstruction and legal paired moves. Stable completion specializes classical 2-SAT \cite{AspvallPlassTarjan1979}. Finite-pool closure specializes classical LP duality \cite{BoydVandenberghe2004} and yields signed real lifts, rather than Boolean endpoints. Polynomial complexity concerns the explicitly represented graph, whose size is exponential in word order.

The arithmetic results use classical cyclotomic tensor relations \cite{MartinReiner2005,DeCarliLaporta2026} and bounded signed-one-hot realization to obtain an all-order rational Farey spectrum and eventual algebraic realization. These are positional embedding statements: raw equalities need not be active selector walls. We use exact algebraic sign evaluation \cite{EmirisTsigaridas2005} and realizable sign arrangements \cite{BjornerEtAl1999}. General root-of-unity relations provide context \cite{Mann1965,ConwayJones1976,LamLeung2000,Steinberger2008,KoleyArikatla2019,ChristieDykemaKlep2020,KissEtAl2026}; these targeted comparisons do not certify exhaustive novelty.
