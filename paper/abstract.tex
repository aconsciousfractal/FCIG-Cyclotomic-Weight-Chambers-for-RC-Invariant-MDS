\begin{abstract}
For DNA order eight, the fixed labelled weights $(s(A),s(C),s(G),s(T))=(-\rho,-1,1,\rho)$ in $\mathcal W$, $\rho>1$, with fixed $A<C<G<T$ ties meet 21 ordinary complete-move components. Their entire union has optimum residual path length 141, including non-RC endpoints. A certified reverse-complement-invariant minimum decycling set of 8230 words instead attains 107 and is optimal in a different component. The atlas classifies $\mathcal W$, not all labelled antisymmetric weight maps. Certified 8232-word RC feedback sets attain length 105; the global cardinality problem remains $8230\le\tau_{\RC}(106)\le8232$.

A two-family selector determines equality strata, and an exact atlas has 86 walls, 173 strata and 114 terminals. Boolean classical retiming optimizes entire components; prescribed RC mate sums give two paired-move sectors, each costing at most one additional path unit. Beyond a component, conditional stable completion is exactly 2-SAT; bounded coordinates and antisymmetric heights describe endpoint and cardinality problems. Balanced closed-walk loads have an exact signed-real finite-pool closure. A certified exterior passes one such relaxation but requires at least 8235 selections in its stated real-gradient class at bound 106, without a global lower-bound claim.

Classical cyclotomic tensor relations and bounded signed-one-hot realization also give an all-order Farey spectrum of rational DNA embedding resonances. A real algebraic ratio is positionally realizable at some even order exactly when it belongs to a cyclotomic field. Complete arithmetic proofs and finite local exclusions are retained in the technical supplement.
\end{abstract}
