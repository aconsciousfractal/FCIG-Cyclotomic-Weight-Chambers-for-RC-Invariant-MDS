\section{Technical supplement: local exclusion domains}

\subsection{What the finite exclusions do and do not prove}
\label{sec:scoped-exclusions}
A PCR group below is either a stable PCR or a pair of nonstable
PCRs interchanged by RC. A chart site is a lower-vertex pair
$\{u,\theta u\}$ in \Cref{thm:bounded-chart}; it is not a PCR
group or a legal move. Changing these definitions changes a
neighbourhood and can invalidate a purported exclusion.

\begin{proposition}[Exact local exclusions]
\label{prop:local-exclusions}
The following finite statements hold for the exact packaged bases.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In the 99-PCR region generated by the displayed critical
cycle of $M_{121}$ and its RC image, with the exterior fixed,
the minimum RC MDS length is 121.
\item Changing at most four RC-PCR groups of $M_{111}$ cannot
produce an RC MDS of length at most 110.
\item Changing at most three RC-PCR groups of $M_{109}$ and
then applying arbitrary integral retiming cannot attain length
at most 108. The minimum in that family is 109. Retiming the
base before measuring the radius is not included.
\item In the chart based at the canonical $M_{107}$, allowing
at most three sites, arbitrary integral site coefficients, all
128 stable bits and an arbitrary integral gradient gives minimum
RC MDS length 107.
\item In each of eight specified regions around $S$ of
\Cref{prop:8232}, with respectively $1,13,139,64,128,256,512,1024$
nonstable groups free and all stable choices unrestricted, the
minimum cardinality at path bound 106 is 8232. These are eight
specified regions, not all regions of those sizes.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Exact finite proof objects]
For the first statement the verifier reconstructs 51 RC-compatible
option domains with 382 initial options and checks 53 walk cuts,
202 option eliminations and a terminal contradiction. No numerical
solver status enters this implication chain.

For the four-group radius, 2486 path or cycle cuts support an
exhaustive branching certificate with 533,138 nodes. Each node
has its declared domain and cut bound recomputed, so complete
branch coverage supplies the exclusion. A two-group change
repairs the old critical cycles but creates a seven-cycle,
showing why PCR and critical-cycle conditions alone are insufficient.
For the three-group radius around $M_{109}$, the 1069 cuts are
all closed walks; their weights are unchanged by subsequent
retiming. The 45,845-node proof thus excludes the full retiming
closure of the stated radius. A separate acyclic three-group
change hits each of the 6,931,456 old critical cycles three
times but has component optimum 111. The exhaustive old-cycle
count uses a tight-clock DAG and is independently replayed.

For three chart sites, globally checked support conditions reduce
the $\binom{8192}{3}=91,592,417,280$ triples to 24,592 necessary
ones. Lifting a rational certificate to an omitted site requires
both vanishing of its combined coefficients and the divisibility
conditions used in row normalization. The verifier checks these
conditions before using a lifted support clause. It reconstructs
the complete support recursion, then checks every surviving triple
by rational Farkas combinations, integer strips, modular equalities
or rounded valid inequalities. There are no unresolved triples.
The 1323 closed walks and all rational coefficients are included;
the checker uses integer and rational arithmetic only.

For the eight regions, deleting all 988 stable-PCR arcs is a valid
relaxation. The verifier checks 844 sequential eliminations of
nonstable options using residual cycles or 107-arc paths. An
option is removed only using already proved residual arcs or
fixed exterior arcs. Every region ends with an empty domain if
all its nonstable PCRs are singly hit. Cost at most 8231 has
this property; the base $S$ attains 8232 in each region.
\end{proof}

The finite cut relaxation for the four-group radius has exact
fractional minimum $1/2$, certified by a feasible rational point
and matching dual multipliers. The corresponding integer cut
system requires at least five changed groups, hence its gap is
at least ten. This concerns that particular relaxation. Adding
more valid inequalities or changing the objective changes the
statement; it is not a universal integrality-gap theorem for
the global problem.

\subsubsection{A boundary clause needs its assumptions}
Each regional elimination proof supplies a set $B$ of exterior
RC literals and a set $\mathcal T$ of nonstable PCR representatives
whose one-selection property the proof used. Writing
$c_P=|M\cap P|$, its valid extension to any RC feedback set at
the target length is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{b\in B}\ind_M(b)+\sum_{P\in\mathcal T}(c_P-1)\ge1.
 \label{eq:regional-surplus}
\end{equation}
Indeed, if the nonnegative integral left side were zero, all
boundary arcs would remain residual and all used PCRs would
be singly hit, so the identical inductive contradiction would
apply. At cost at most 8231 the nonstable surplus vanishes,
and the bare boundary clause is valid. At higher cost it need
not vanish. This surplus is different from the stable-PCR
surplus in \Cref{cor:surplus-two-arm}.

The shortest supplied boundary clause has 21 RC literals and
uses 12 group assumptions and eight cycles. Both $S_A,S_B$
satisfy all eight bare clauses while moving the double-PCR
surplus to a different nonstable group. Thus these clauses
are useful necessary constraints, not global refutations.
The companion also checks 214 globally valid closed-walk cuts
at length 106, of which 187 require one hit and 27 require two.
Their validity does not imply their sufficiency.

\subsubsection{Reproducibility and scope}
The companion retains the literal inputs and exact proof objects for these
local exclusions. Its separate base-four checks recompute the positive
endpoints and sharp parity example; small-instance tests exercise the
conditional stable-completion and lifted two-arm implementations. Such
checks support implementations; the uniform results depend on their proofs.
Each exclusion is tied to the stated base, exterior, neighbourhood or affine
family. None certifies the missing global optimum.
