# Exact open problems — version 3.2.0

1. **Global RC DNA optimum at k=8.** The current MDS has L=107 and exact component optimum 107. A target at most 106 must change component and hit its one-hit 108-cycle at least twice. The stable fibre and three-site domain are already excluded.
2. **RC cardinality at length 106.** The exact value lies in {8230,8231,8232}; 8232 already attains 105. Cost 8231 has exactly one double stable PCR. The fixed-exterior 495-branch oracle is complete, but the nonstable choices remain global. Eight regional and three affine-pruning exclusions do not close them.
3. **Uniform component changes.** The finite DNA improvements do not supply an all-even-order improvement theorem. The complete chart supplies coordinates, not a uniformly good solution.
4. **Fixed-order algebraic realization.** Minimal realizing order, conductor bounds and general active algebraic midpoint branches remain open; eventual cyclotomic realization is proved.
5. **Other labelled weights and higher-order arrangements.** The 21-component atlas at k=8 covers only W=(-rho,-1,1,rho), rho>1, with fixed A<C<G<T ties. Other injective antisymmetric sign/order assignments can meet additional components; their union optimum is not classified. The minimal fixed-sign descriptions at DNA order eight are not general classifications for all orders, larger weight spaces or adaptive encodings.
6. **Mixed-wall length bounds.** The exact max-plus formula and finite equality checks do not establish uniform alignment or a width-two bound at mixed walls.
7. **Both RC sectors simultaneously.** The possible one-unit cost is now attained in one prescribed sector. It remains unknown here whether both sectors can be worse than the ordinary optimum.
8. **Finite-pool refinement and joint feasibility.** Passing a finite mixed-walk pool is only a necessary signed-real test. The strictness example is already excluded throughout its nonstable real-gradient class; additional effort on that same class cannot produce an MDS improvement. Useful new constraints must address genuinely surviving exteriors or their global Boolean coupling.
9. **Component ranges and qualified symmetries.** Minimum optimization does not determine component maxima or validate naive Galois transport of terminals.

Next research should declare one target and one domain not already excluded. Produce an exact endpoint or a complete certificate for that domain; a bounded solver timeout remains an inconclusive experiment. No additional discovery search is needed merely to verify this paper.

Biological or software advantages require separate comparative benchmarks. Odd-order problems keep their own hypotheses.
