# Public claim boundary — version 3.2.0

The manuscript distinguishes written general proofs from finite certificates. The original cyclotomic, positional, rational-spectrum, chamber and retiming results retain their stated hypotheses and imported sources.

Fix the labelled DNA family W=(-rho,-1,1,rho), rho>1, in the order (A,C,G,T), with the declared lexicographic A<C<G<T tie rules. At k=8 it encounters exactly 21 ordinary components and has optimum 141 even over their entire union, including non-RC endpoints. These claims do not classify all injective antisymmetric labelled weights. Other sign/order assignments with ties fixed can enter further components; their complete atlas and optimal union length are not established here. The initial explicit construction at 131 is preserved. The current RC MDS at 107 has 8230 words and a certified exact ordinary-component optimum of 107. This gives a global upper bound, not global optimality.

The one-unit cost in a prescribed RC sector is sharp: a component has ordinary optimum 124 and sector optima 125 and 124. Simultaneous loss in both sectors is not resolved.

The bounded signed chart is complete for even q,k with free complement and a base RC one-per-PCR selection. It uses integral coordinates and an integral clock. The Boolean endpoint rank relaxation does not justify relaxing that signed clock.

Stable completion is polynomial only after fixing the nonstable choices and measuring size by the explicitly enumerated graph. It does not optimize all nonstable choices, count all 2-SAT models in polynomial time, or run in time polynomial in word order. The excess-one extension has exactly 495 branches for DNA k=8.

Sets of 8232 words with length 105 prove 8230 <= tau_RC(106) <= 8232. The first is inclusion-minimal even without RC, which does not mean minimum cardinality. Single-word augmentations of size 8231 and length 106 in the comparison are not RC.

The 99-PCR region, radius-four around M111, radius-three followed by retiming around M109, three chart sites around the canonical M107, eight named regions, and three affine-pruning families are exact finite domains. They do not cover all bases, all regions of those sizes, or unrestricted new insertions. The three-site domain allows all stable bits, arbitrary integral coefficients and arbitrary integral retiming.

Stable two-arm surplus and nonstable regional surplus are different terms. Removing their assumptions or copying reduced MDS rows into a surplus model is not justified. The 214 global cycle inequalities are necessary, not proved sufficient.

Balanced-load projection uses the uncapped aggregate stable budget c_P >= 1, sum c_P <= N_S+b with b >= 0. The closure theorem concerns a finite mixed pool; signed lifts need not be integral, nonnegative or Boolean. Pure nonstable walks are separate constraints. The 1804-walk example passes at b=0, but new returns imply a lower bound 8235 throughout one nonstable real-gradient class; this bound is not asserted to be attained. Its forty returns have lengths 52–110, and their closed walks have lengths 53–111. This class is not an ordinary complete-move component.

No global optimum, exhaustive novelty, biological performance, literature-wide record, proof-assistant certification, specialist acceptance, publication or submission is asserted.
