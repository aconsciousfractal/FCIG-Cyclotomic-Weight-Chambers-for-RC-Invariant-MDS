# Contribution and version 3.2.0

The article follows a single chain from a specified weight family to component optimization, a construction outside those components, and the limitations of necessary tests for further improvement.

| Contribution | Exact scope | Support |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Complete DNA8 atlas | W=(-rho,-1,1,rho), rho>1, in labelled order (A,C,G,T), fixed A<C<G<T ties | 86 walls, 173 strata, 114 terminals and 21 ordinary components |

| Balanced finite-pool closure | Uncapped aggregate stable budget b>=0; signed real lifts | Normal form and finite LP duality proof |
| Strictness of one necessary test | One 8102-arc core passes 1804 walks; forty returns force class cost>=8235 | Exact lift and return certificates; no global bound or attainment claim |
| Arithmetic of the chambers | Rational Farey spectrum, positional counts and eventual cyclotomic realization | Complete proofs retained in the technical supplement |

The 141 barrier is not asserted for all injective antisymmetric DNA weight maps. Changing labelled signs/orders without transporting tie rules can change the ordinary component. The article includes a small exact example explaining this distinction.

Version 3.2.0 makes the domain explicit throughout, reorganizes the exposition into eight main sections, removes a repeated sine-basis proof, and gives a direct companion locator with an immutable evidence identity. Technical proofs and scientifically necessary historical certificates are preserved. The only changed scientific-capsule payload is an unused provenance field removed without changing its other fields; three unused historical report builders are omitted, with packaging provenance updated.

The constructive frontier is unchanged: 8230 words attain 107, 8232 attain 105, and 8230<=tau_RC(106)<=8232. This revision neither runs a new global search nor settles specialist novelty. Classical cyclotomic, retiming, SCC, 2-SAT, ordering and LP results remain explicitly attributed.
