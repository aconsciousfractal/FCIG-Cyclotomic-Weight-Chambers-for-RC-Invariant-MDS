\section{Technical supplement: the earlier length-131 escape}

\subsection{An earlier length-131 escape construction}
\label{sec:construction-131}
This retained construction documents one explicit route out of the weighted
components. Its upper bound is superseded by \Cref{thm:construction-107}.

For a lower word $u$ and a nonempty proper letter set $B$, consider
the simultaneous cochain change
\[
 \Delta_{u,B}=\sum_{a\in B}(\ind_{ua}-\ind_{au})
       +J\!\left(\sum_{a\in B}(\ind_{ua}-\ind_{au})\right).
\]
It preserves RC and the sum on every PCR. It does not by itself
guarantee Boolean indicators or acyclicity; both must be verified.

\begin{proposition}[Four changes of component]\label{prop:construction-131}
Start with the weighted terminal $M_0$ for $\rho=49/16$
(row \texttt{k8\_c094}). Apply the four changes in
\Cref{tab:patches} in order. All five endpoints are RC MDS
of size 8230, with
\[
 \boxed{L(M_0),\ldots,L(M_4)=141,135,135,133,131.}
\]
They occupy five distinct ordinary components and each attains
the optimum in its own component. The last component is distinct
from all 21 in \Cref{tab:component-optima}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Exact certificates]
Direct reconstruction checks all $4^8$ membership bits after each
patch, and checks RC, one word per PCR and acyclicity. Integer
ranks and explicit longest paths give the upper and attained
bounds. One-hit simple cycles of lengths $142,136,136,134,132$
give the matching component lower bounds. Ten cycle separators
distinguish all pairs of endpoints; another 21 distinguish the
last endpoint from the weighted components. Each separator has
at most 15 arcs. The separate checker tests these claims without
importing the discovery search.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Global upper bound, exact component optimum]
\label{cor:global-upper-131}
For RC DNA MDS at $k=8$,
\[
 \min_{M\ {\rm RC\ MDS}}L(M)\le131,\qquad
 \min_{N\in\CF(M_4)}L(N)=131.
\]
The final upper residual has length 130 in edges. Its longest
avoiding word has 138 letters, so every DNA word of length 139
contains a selected eight-letter window.
\end{corollary}

The final one-hit cycle is the cyclic concatenation of these lines:
\begin{center}\ttfamily\small

AGCAGCCGAGGAGGATAGGCCTATCCTCCTCGGCTGCTGGTCTTCTTGTG\\
TTGTTTTTTTGTTCTTGGGTGCTGGGCTGATG

Its length is 132, its seven-letter prefixes are distinct, and its
only selected cyclic eight-letter window is \texttt{GCGCGGAG}
at zero-based index 131. Deleting that arc leaves 131 residual
arcs. The machine-readable string in
\pathfile{cycle_132_compact.json} is checked against the cycle
certificate. Any improvement to $L\le130$ must hit this cycle at
least twice, and therefore leave this component.

\subsubsection{Reproducibility and evidence boundary}
The public package contains the original 30-row regression capsule,
the complete 199-row atlas, mixed-wall and component certificates,
and the retiming construction. The atlas checks 11,432,704 word
decisions. The retiming checker verifies 72 certificate occurrences
(not 72 distinct sets), 42 retimings, 30 lower-bound cycles and
31 short separators. Its 24 semantic mutations include missing
lower bounds and a duplicated parity sector. The atlas and wall
checkers have 18 and 15 further mutation controls respectively.
Small exhaustive tests cover $(q,k)=(2,2),(2,4),(4,2)$ and 64
RC-feasibility queries, plus 260 bounded autodual ranks.

All finite outputs have a declared scope. A rank certificate proves
a universal path bound for its finite DAG; a completed list proves
a complete atlas only because the affine-root reduction is
exhaustive. None of these checks proves an all-order theorem by
sampling. Distinct implementations remain AI-assisted checks,
not independent human acceptance. No timeout or incomplete
search is used to claim that a shorter global construction is
impossible.
