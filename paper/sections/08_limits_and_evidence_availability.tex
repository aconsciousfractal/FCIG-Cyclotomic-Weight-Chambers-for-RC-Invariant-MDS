\section{Limits, open questions, and evidence availability}
\label{sec:boundaries}
The all-order rational embedding spectrum, rational twice-prime
classifications, complete fixed-labelled-$\mathcal W$ selector $k=8$ atlas, ordinary
component optimization and existence of exactly two RC sectors
are established, together with sharp fixed-sector cost, a complete
bounded chart, conditional stable completion and exact balanced finite-pool
closure. The latter remains a relaxation with signed real lifts. The following
questions remain.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Global DNA optimum at $k=8$.}
The construction has $L=107$, and 107 is its exact component
optimum. The minimum over all RC MDS is unknown.
Any $L\le106$ construction must leave that component and hit the
displayed 108-cycle at least twice. This necessary condition
is not a sufficient construction or a global lower bound of 107.
The 128-member stable fibre and the three-site chart domain are
already closed at this target. Searching those same domains again
cannot improve the result.

\item \textbf{Exact cardinality at path bound 106.}
We have $8230\le\tau_{\RC}(106)\le8232$, with cost 8232 already
attaining length 105. Cost 8231 would require exactly one double
stable PCR; the 495-branch completion theorem solves its stable
subproblem only after the nonstable choices are fixed. The eight
regional exclusions and three affine-pruning exhaustions are not
global exclusions. The real-gradient-class lower in
\Cref{prop:core-pool-strictness} is likewise local to its stated affine class.
A new insertion or a change of nonstable component may escape these domains.

\item \textbf{Uniform changes of component.}
The four patches are certified at $k=8$. No theorem supplies
analogous improvements at arbitrary even order, jointly
preserving Boolean membership, RC, PCR counts and acyclicity.

\item \textbf{Fixed-order algebraic realization and active branches.}
The cyclotomic-field condition is sufficient when the order
may grow. At a prescribed order the full positional image,
minimal realizing order and efficient conductor bounds remain
unclassified. Eventual raw midpoint witnesses can have lambda
zero; general algebraic active midpoint realization is open.
The resolved quadratic power-of-two slice is not the full
higher-order domain.

\item \textbf{Higher-order terminal and component arrangements.}
At $k=8$, 86 fixed linear signs determine the terminal and ten
determine its encountered component, both minimally. General
orders and higher-dimensional weight spaces lack such a minimal
classification here. Adaptive or nonlinear encodings are distinct
questions. Other labelled sign/order assignments already at $k=8$ are outside $\mathcal W$ and their terminal and component arrangements are not classified here; no exclusion of all such weight maps follows from the 21-component atlas.

\item \textbf{Length control at mixed walls.}
Regular walls have width at most two. The mixed-wall indicator
formula does not imply a comparable uniform path-length bound;
a width-20 example is certified. The max-plus formula is exact,
but equality in $L\le2L_P+L_N+2$ is verified only in the finite
atlas and still needs a uniform alignment argument.

\item \textbf{Simultaneous cost in both RC sectors.}
Each sector optimum is $\ceil R-1$ or $\ceil R$.
The extra unit is now attained in one sector, while the other
sector in that example attains the ordinary optimum. Whether
both sectors of some ordinary component require the extra unit
remains unresolved here. All 42 sectors from the fixed-labelled-$\mathcal W$ atlas
still attain their ordinary optima.

\item \textbf{Component range and qualified symmetries.}
The minimum does not determine the maximum in a component or
settle the component-range questions of
\cite{MarcaisDeBlasioKingsford2024}. Naive Galois descent to
terminals is false. A useful restricted transport theorem must
explicitly preserve positivity, positional data and ties.
\end{enumerate}

None of these questions is an unproved premise of the established
results. Biological and software benefits require comparative
benchmarks. Odd-order constrained decycling and packing identities
are separate problems. The general height theorem permits fixed
vertices, but this does not remove the even-order hypotheses of
the PCR chart, paired-sector and stable-completion applications.
