# Accessibility boundary

The selected PDF provides:

- extractable text;
- a table of contents and PDF bookmarks;
- internal and bibliographic hyperlinks; and
- embedded fonts.

The PDF is not tagged and is not asserted to conform to PDF/UA. Complete
LaTeX source is included as the accessible source representation. A venue
requiring tagged PDF output should generate and separately validate an
accessible edition.

No meaning in the article depends only on colour. Tables and equations are
available in source form.
